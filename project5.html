Role Controls & Aerodynamics Researcher

Ongoing UROP research on a stop-rotor VTOL aircraft in Prof. Ian Hunter's Bioinstrumentation Lab, advised by Denzel Carter and funded by the Paul E. Gray (1954) UROP Fund. The work spans flight controls and aerodynamics, ahead of an upcoming research publication.

Control Systems

I redesigned the swashplate actuator configuration in SolidWorks for increased rigidity, running a motion study to verify actuator clearances across the full range of travel. I built a swashplate controller implementing force-to-command decomposition, CCPM mixing, and servo-horn linearization, then validated the main rotor and swashplate CCPM logic through hardware-in-the-loop testing on Pixhawk 6X flight hardware, running the controller against a Simulink plant model.

Aerodynamics

I'm leading wind tunnel testing and data acquisition for the aerodynamic evaluation of a set of novel VTOL airfoils in MIT AeroAstro's 2 to 3 ft tunnel, in support of an ICRA 2027 submission.

Wind Tunnel Testing

I designed wake-rake and manometer measurement procedures in consultation with Prof. Mark Drela, author of XFOIL. I applied the Jones momentum-integral method to extract profile drag alongside lift measurements, supporting CFD analysis.

This project involves unpublished research, so I'm keeping the details shared here intentionally limited to protect that work ahead of publication. If you'd like to learn more, feel free to reach out directly, I'm happy to discuss specifics one-on-one.
