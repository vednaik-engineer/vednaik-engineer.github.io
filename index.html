About Me

I'm an MIT student athlete studying Mechanical Engineering, with a focus on structural design, manufacturing, and the mechanisms that make hardware actually work.

I care about the same process wherever it shows up: modeling something, working out whether it'll hold up, building it, and testing it to see if the math was right. I'm especially drawn to mechatronic, integrated systems, the instrumentation and data acquisition that make sense of how they perform, and above all, innovative designs and systems that challenge current engineering knowledge.
